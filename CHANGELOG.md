## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-14

First public preview.

### Added

- 6-operator FM synth with role-based macros: Brightness, Bite, Grit and Body.
- The full operator panel, the 32 DX7 algorithms, stereo detune, a resonant filter and a chorus.
- Pressure and timbre built in as expression.
- MIDI 2.0 notes taken directly, so a Pitch 7.9 note plays its exact pitch; MPE and CLAP note
  expressions too.
- CLAP plugin for Linux (x86-64) and macOS 26 or later (Apple Silicon).
